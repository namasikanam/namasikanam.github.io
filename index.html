About Me

I am a PhD student at MPI-SP, fortunately supervised by Prof. Gilles Barthe. I'm interested in formal verification, especially its application to security, and, of course, like everyone nowadays, its interplay with machine learning.

I received my bachelor’s degree in 2021 and my master’s degree in 2024 from Tsinghua University. During the time, I did internships at Megvii and RealAI, and involved in Closed Social.

I was a competitive programmer during 2011-2021, winning several gold medals including NOI 2016. I was also involved in the organization of several programming competitions, including THUPC 2019-2021.

I am cynical. I co-organized a satirical public activity, Huatsing Special Ordinary Scholarship (华清大学特普通奖学金) in 2020-2021. We're mocking Special Scholarship (for the top-10 students) in Tsinghua University. Why don't ordinary students deserve to be awarded? I plan to re-set-up our official website, but I am very lazy, so for the moment, if you are interested, you can search on the Internet in Chinese. The following Chinese media coverage is based on direct interviews with us: [清新传媒](https://qxcm.tsjc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pc/folder120/folder127/2021-11-18/XqCKNJ3LLprhRTGE.html), [真实故事计划 (Top 10 in 2022)](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/556462192).

I am nerdy, honestly nerdier than ChatGPT. I am learning how to interact with people, learning the relationships, and learning to understand and take care of the emotion of myself and others. I hope one day I will have the courage and ability to interact with the world by an authentic self.
