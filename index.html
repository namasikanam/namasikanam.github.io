About Me

I am a PhD student at MPI-SP, fortunately supervised by Gilles Barthe. I'm interested in formal verification, especially its application to security, and, of course, like everyone, its interplay with machine learning.

I received my bachelor degree in 2021 and master degree in 2024 from Tsinghua University. I did internships at Megvii and RealAI.

I was a competitive programmer during 2011-2021, winning several gold medals including the Chinese national contest, NOI 2016. I was really into the tricks in competitive programming, but there seem to be nothing deep, but.

I am cynical, which can be shown by a satirical public activity I co-organized, Huatsing Special Ordinary Scholarship (华清大学特普通奖学金) in 2020 and 2021, which is against Special Scholarship (for the top-10 students) in Tsinghua University. I plan to re-set-up our official website, but I am very lazy, so for the moment, if you are interested, you can search in Chinese.

I am nerdy, nerdier than ChatGPT. I am still learning how to interact with people, learning the relationships, and learning to understand and take care of the emotion of myself and others. I hope one day I will have the courage and ability to interact with the world by an authentic self.
