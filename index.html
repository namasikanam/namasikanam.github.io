About Me

I am a PhD student at MPI-SP, fortunately supervised by Gilles Barthe. I'm interested in formal verification, especially its application to security, and, of course, like everyone, its interplay with machine learning.

I received my bachelor degree in 2021 and master degree in 2024 from Tsinghua University. I was a competitive programmer during 2011-2021, winning several gold medals including NOI 2016. I am a bit cynical, which can be shown by a satirical public activity I co-organized, Huatsing Special Ordinary Scholarship 华清大学特普通奖学金 in 2020 and 2021, which is against Special Scholarship (for the top-10 students) in Tsinghua University. I plan to re-set-up our official website, but I'm very lazy, so for the moment, if you're interested, you can search in Chinese.

I often feel I'm very nerdy, nerdier than ChatGPT. I'm learning how to interact with people. I'm learning to understand and take care of the emotion of myself and others. I hope one day I will have the courage to interact with the world by an authentic self.
